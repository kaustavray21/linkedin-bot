I don't think boAt did anything wrong by selling Chinese products with their name 
because don't you all have an iPhone with you?
 
I remember promising my nephew a phone if he got good results in class 10. 
He's smart and I had already ordered an iPhone for him before he even told me. 
 
When I asked which phone he wants, he said iPhone 17. Without a second of hesitation.
 
Not because I know him well. Because I understand the psychology of brands. 
He wants to walk into college feeling “cool” like a “main character”.
The iPhone is just the vehicle.
 
All of Apple's products are assembled in China. Every single one. And we love Apple for it.
 
We buy a product because we need it.
But we buy 1 product out of 100 because of how it makes us feel.
 
Yes, boAt's marketing team did mess up with the "Made in India" positioning. 
 
You can hate them for that. But the way they built a brand that made Indians feel cool buying Indian 
That is a masterclass every D2C brand in India is still studying.
 
Because while we were cancelling boAt, we were wearing Nike stitched in Chinese factories and buying H&M hauls from the same supply chains.
 
The charger on your desk China. Your power bank China. The Diwali lights China.
boAt needed the C word. But that word is not cancellation It is correction.
 
boAt sinking doesn't tell us they failed. 
It tells us how unaware we are of our own contradictions.
