5 DM openers that actually get replies (without pitching)

Most people lose the reply
before the conversation even starts.

Use these as-is.

1. “Quick question {name}. are you currently handling your own lead gen or delegating it?”

why it works
yes or no answer
zero pressure
relevant to almost every founder

2. “Hey {name}, noticed you work with {their niche}. what’s been your most reliable source of clients so far?”

why it works
people love talking about what already works
it opens context, not defense

3. “Random one {name}. are you actively trying to grow right now, or just maintaining?”

why it works
respects timing
doesn’t assume pain
puts them in control

4. “Curious {name}. what made you choose {their current offer/positioning} over {obvious alternative}?”

why it works
shows real observation
forces a thoughtful reply
instantly separates you from spam

5. “not pitching anything {name}. just wanted to ask, what’s the biggest bottleneck in your business right now?”

why it works
removes fear upfront
invites honesty
starts real conversations

Important rule most people miss

Dm one is not for selling.
it’s for permission.

no pitch.
no links.
no calendar.

If they reply
you’ve already won.

Ps. if your dm starts with a paragraph,
you’re not doing outbound.

you’re begging for attention.

which one would you actually reply to?
