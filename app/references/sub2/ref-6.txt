How to stop sounding like chatgpt in linkedin posts

Most people don’t sound robotic
because ai is bad.

They sound robotic
because they give ai nothing real.

ai doesn’t make content fake.
lack of context does.

Here’s the shift that fixed it for me.

I stopped asking ai to “write a linkedin post”
and started feeding it my life.

What actually works

Before you ask ai to write anything,
give it these 5 things.

1. Real moment
tell it what actually happened.

not
“write about consistency”

But
“i was sitting in my room at 2am, broke, staring at my calendar with zero calls booked”

Raw moments create raw output.

2. Your opinion
not a lesson.

ai defaults to teaching.
you need to give it judgment.

Tell it
“i think most people give trash advice here”

or
“this made me angry”

or
“i realized i was lying to myself”

opinions kill generic tone.

3. What you would never say out loud
this is the cheat code.

Tell ai the insecure thought.
the petty thought.
the uncomfortable truth.

Example

“i was jealous of people winning while i felt stuck”

that’s what makes it human.

4. How you actually talk
not how you think you should sound.

Tell ai
“write like i’m talking to a friend, not teaching a class”

or

“short lines, casual tone, no motivation talk”
ai mirrors instructions perfectly.

5. Who this is for
one person. not everyone.

Tell ai
“this is for a 22-year-old agency owner making 80k a month and panicking”

clarity sharpens voice.

simple prompt structure i use

“this is a real situation that happened to me:
{story}
this is my honest take on it:
{opinion}

this is the uncomfortable part i don’t usually say:
{truth}

Write this like a linkedin post.
short lines.

no generic advice.
sound like me, not chatgpt.”
that’s it.

Ai doesn’t kill authenticity.
lazy prompting does.

Ps. if your post sounds like everyone else,
It’s because you gave ai the same inputs as everyone else.
