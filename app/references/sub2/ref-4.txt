personal branding is overrated.

wild thing to say

for someone who runs a personal branding agency.

but it’s true.
most people today
aren’t building a personal brand.

they’re building a content machine.

no opinions
no stories
no personality

just…
post → hook → carousel → repeat

and yeah, it works.

you’ll get views
followers
maybe even some vanity engagement

but here’s the real test:
say their name in a room full of people.

no one remembers them.
because they never gave people
a reason to remember them.

on the flip side…
there are people

who are brutally honest online
who share things that actually happened
who say things they actually believe

they don’t go viral every day.
but when they show up?

people listen.
people remember.
people trust.
and trust compounds.

i’ve seen this first hand.
on instagram…

less than 6.5k followers
no crazy viral content
but 10,000+ DMs.

not “nice post bro”

real questions
real conversations
real intent

and that’s where everything changed:
→ biggest names became clients
→ inbound never stopped
→ opportunities came without chasing

because i wasn’t trying to be “a creator”
i was just being… recognisable.

here’s the truth no one talks about:
views get you attention

personality gets you remembered
but only trust gets you paid

so yeah,
personal branding is overrated…
if you’re just creating content.

but if you’re building
a real identity people can feel?

that’s the most unfair advantage you can have.

P.S.
next time you scroll LinkedIn,
ask yourself:

would i remember this person
if their name came up tomorrow?
