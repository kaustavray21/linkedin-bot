The perfect weekly posting mix for founders who want booked calls

You don’t need to post 10 times a week.

You don’t need daily motivation.
You don’t need carousels every day.

3 to 4 posts per week is perfect
If you post the right things.

Here’s the mix that actually drives calls.

post type 1
personal stories, experiences, learnings
1 to 2 posts per week

This is where trust is built.

What did you try?
What failed?
What surprised you?
What changed your thinking?

not lessons.
not frameworks.

real moments.

People don’t book calls with experts.
They book calls with people they relate to.

post type 2
case studies, testimonials, client wins
1 post per week

This is proof.

before and after.
What was broken?
what changed.
What result came out?

no hype.
No fake numbers.

just reality.

This answers the silent question in their head
“Will this work for someone like me?”

post type 3
breakdowns, industry comments, opinions
1 post per week

This is authority.

Comment on what everyone is doing wrong.
break down why something isn’t working anymore.
Share how you think about a problem differently.

not teaching.
not step-by-step.

your lens.
An 
important part that most founders miss

Even education should come from these angles.

not
“Here’s how to do outbound.”

but
Here’ss why my outbound failed before this.”

not
“content strategy tips”

but
Why my content didn’t convert for 90 days?”

Stories first.
opinions second.
lessons implied.

That’s how content turns into conversations.

ps.If your content is helpful but forgettable,
It’s because it teaches without context.
